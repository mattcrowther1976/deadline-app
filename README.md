# DEADLINE

A clickable prototype of DEADLINE, a supercar spotting app made by Duke Crowther.

**Try it:** https://mattcrowther1976.github.io/deadline-app/

- `docs/` is the website: the prototype screens plus a small runtime that plays them in the browser.
- `design/` holds the original design files and photos.

## Photos

The car and founder photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under Creative Commons licences (mostly CC BY-SA 4.0), or are in the public domain. Photographers and licences are listed on the [photo credits page](https://mattcrowther1976.github.io/deadline-app/credits.html). Shared photos stay under their original licences.

Car makers' names are used for identification only. DEADLINE is not affiliated with any car maker, and the car stats in the prototype are sample figures.
